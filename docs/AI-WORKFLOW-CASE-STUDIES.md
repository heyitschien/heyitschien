## Flagship 1 — Can AI Yet?

**What it is**  
An early-alpha AI capability evaluation lab that tests whether agents and tool
integrations can perform real business workflows reliably—using bounded
contracts, evals, expected-versus-actual checks, machine receipts, independent
review, and explicit proof states.

**My responsibility**  
I define the evaluation contracts, sequence HubSpot and environment validation
work, insist on machine evidence over persuasive narrative, run correction
loops after independent review, and refuse to upgrade proof states without
accepted receipts.

**Distinct decisions**

- preserve PROVEN, DRY-CERTIFIED, CONFIGURED_NOT_LIVE_PROVEN, BLOCKED, and
  NOT_PROVEN as non-interchangeable labels;
- treat dry certification as preparation, not live proof;
- fail closed when world-state checks do not match expectations;
- keep secrets, portal identifiers, and private packets out of public claims.

**Evidence**

| Artifact | Link |
| --- | --- |
| Public-safe summary | [CAN-AI-YET-CASE-STUDY.md](CAN-AI-YET-CASE-STUDY.md) |
| Public case study | [can-ai-yet-case-study](https://github.com/heyitschien/can-ai-yet-case-study) |
| Live site / methodology | [canaiyet.com](https://canaiyet.com) · [methodology](https://canaiyet.com/methodology) |
| Evidence and boundaries | [Current bounded claims](https://github.com/heyitschien/can-ai-yet-case-study/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE-AND-BOUNDARIES.md) |

**Result**  
Public proof that I can evaluate AI and integrations with the same discipline
used for real implementation work: define, test, capture evidence, correct, and
label what remains unproven.

**Limitation**  
Lab and bounded environment proof—not an enterprise adoption case study, not a
revenue claim, and not a claim that every HubSpot CAP-001 path is live-proven
end to end.

**Lesson**  
The valuable AI implementation skill is knowing exactly what evidence supports
a claim—and saying so out loud.

## Flagship 2 — Paid Client Implementation

**What it is**  
A paid independent real-estate web and lead-workflow engagement: public
presence, client-owned GitHub-to-Vercel delivery, consultation intake into
Google Sheets, reliability protections, seller-guide enablement surfaces,
validation, and documented handoff.

**My responsibility**  
I owned discovery, requirements translation, configuration/integration,
validation, client enablement, documentation, and continued iteration—not a
one-off website dump.

**Distinct decisions**

- treat the operational problem as lead reliability and client operability,
  not page decoration;
- keep deployment and lead destination client-owned;
- prove production lead delivery and duplicate-submission protection;
- separate completed work from open client-owned dependencies.

**Evidence**

| Artifact | Link |
| --- | --- |
| Public case study | [PAID-CLIENT-IMPLEMENTATION-CASE-STUDY.md](PAID-CLIENT-IMPLEMENTATION-CASE-STUDY.md) |
| Implementation loop | [IMPLEMENTATION-AI-SYSTEMS.md](IMPLEMENTATION-AI-SYSTEMS.md) |

**Result**  
Real external-customer proof of implementation and technical-solutions work:
understand the workflow, make it operable, prove it, and hand it off cleanly.

**Limitation**  
Public-safe and anonymized—client identity, private sheet data, secrets, and
unverified business-impact metrics are omitted.

**Lesson**  
Strong implementation evidence looks like a production path a real person can
keep operating.

## Flagship 3 — Hack for LA · TDM Calculator

**What it is**  
A volunteer contribution to Hack for LA's React/Vite TDM Calculator in an
existing team codebase. It is public contribution evidence, not employment.

**My responsibility**  
I implemented scoped front-end work, responded to repository constraints and
peer review, and carried the work through the upstream contribution process.

**Distinct decisions**

- work inside the established architecture rather than inventing a parallel
  project;
- keep the contribution small enough to review and validate;
- use upstream pull requests as the evidence source;
- describe merged and under-review work separately.

**Evidence**

| Artifact | Link |
| --- | --- |
| Merged contribution | [PR #3531](https://github.com/hackforla/tdm-calculator/pull/3531) |
| Current work under review | [PR #3576](https://github.com/hackforla/tdm-calculator/pull/3576) |

**Result**  
Public proof of scoped implementation in a real team repository, automated
checks, peer review, and an upstream merge.

**Limitation**  
Volunteer contribution—not employment, project leadership, or ownership of
the broader TDM Calculator.

**Lesson**  
Team engineering evidence is strongest when the scope, review discussion,
checks, and final upstream state are inspectable.

## Flagship 4 — Autonomous Trading Systems Lab

**What it is**  
A public-safe case study of complex systems work: bounded requirements,
sequenced integrations, observable validation, documented ownership, and human
review before consequential actions.

**My responsibility**  
I structured the system boundaries, evidence expectations, and human-gate
posture so research complexity does not become unverifiable claims.

**Distinct decisions**

- keep research documentation separate from production financial claims;
- require observable validation and ownership clarity;
- keep consequential actions under human review.

**Evidence**

**Result**  
Proof that I can handle complex systems work with requirements discipline and
validation gates.

**Limitation**  
Research documentation—not a production financial system, performance claim,

**Lesson**  
Complexity becomes trustworthy only when boundaries, evidence, and human
ownership stay explicit.

## Flagship 5 — Cousin Radio

**What it is**  
A shipped family music product—live at
[cousinradio.com](https://cousinradio.com)—with mobile-first discovery,
persistent playback, deployment ownership, and ongoing iteration.

**My responsibility**  
I led product direction, clarified the family use case, made scope and
architecture decisions, coordinated implementation, reviewed behavior, and
managed deployment concerns.

**Distinct decisions**

- prioritize a mobile-first family experience;
- separate public exploration from private/family controls;
- preserve persistent playback across navigation;
- use staged validation and branch protection rather than uncontrolled changes.

**Evidence**

| Artifact | Link |
| --- | --- |
| Live product | [cousinradio.com](https://cousinradio.com) |
| Employer proof | [EMPLOYER-PROOF.md](https://github.com/heyitschien/cousin-radio/blob/main/docs/EMPLOYER-PROOF.md) |
| Public showcase | [cousin-radio](https://github.com/heyitschien/cousin-radio) |

**Result**  
A real deployed product rather than a static demo.

**Limitation**  
Family product—not an enterprise SaaS case study or evidence of SaaS support
employment.

**Lesson**  
AI can accelerate implementation; product coherence still depends on sustained
human decisions about users, scope, quality, and integration.
